Argo for Frigate

Support

The fastest way to reach us is email: support@inceptyon.io. A real person reads it, usually within a day or two.

What do I need?

A Frigate NVR server that your iPhone or iPad can reach: on your home Wi-Fi, or away from home over a VPN such as Tailscale. Argo is tested with Frigate 0.18. Everyone in the house signs in with their own Frigate login, which whoever runs Frigate can add in Frigate's settings.

Can I try it without a server?

Yes. On the sign-in screen, tap Try it with sample cameras. You can leave the demo from Settings at any time.

What address do I type?

The address you open Frigate at, such as 192.168.1.20 or frigate.local. Argo signs in over https on Frigate's login port, 8971. If your Frigate uses its own certificate, Argo shows its fingerprint and asks you to trust it once; after that it refuses any other certificate.

Notifications don't arrive

  • Frigate's own notifications must be on: in Frigate, open Settings, then Notifications.
  • Frigate reads new notification sign-ups only when it starts. After turning notifications on in Argo, restart Frigate (admins can do it from Argo's Settings).
  • The Frigate server needs internet access to send alerts.
  • Check that notifications are allowed for Argo in iOS Settings.

Two-way talk doesn't work

The camera needs a speaker that go2rtc can reach (its "backchannel"), and Frigate's go2rtc.webrtc.candidates must list an address your device can reach on port 8555, the same setup talk needs in Frigate's own page.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Video and snapshots go only between your device and your Frigate server. Details in the privacy policy.

Is Argo made by the Frigate team?

No. Argo is an independent app by Inceptyon Labs. Frigate's own web interface stays the place to set up cameras, zones and users; Argo is for watching, alerts and playback.
