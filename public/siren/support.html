Support

The fastest way to reach us is email: support@inceptyon.io. A real person reads it, usually within a day or two.

What do I need?

An Audiobookshelf server with your audiobooks, your own or one someone in your household runs, and an account on it. At home, Siren reaches the server over your Wi-Fi. Away from home, the server needs an address you can reach from outside, or a VPN back to it. Books you download keep playing without the server.

Signing in with OpenID Connect

If your server uses single sign-on, add siren://oauth to Allowed Mobile Redirect URIs under Settings → Authentication on the server, then sign in from Siren.

Is my listening sent anywhere?

Not to us. Siren talks only to your Audiobookshelf server, and nothing goes to Inceptyon Labs. Details in the privacy policy.

Is Siren made by Audiobookshelf?

No. Siren is an independent app by Inceptyon Labs and is not made or endorsed by the Audiobookshelf project.
