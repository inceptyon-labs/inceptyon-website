Support

The fastest way to reach us is email: support@inceptyon.io. A real person reads it, usually within a day or two.

What do I need?

A Plex account and a Plex Media Server with your movies and shows, your own or one a friend shares with you. At home, Ithaca reaches the server over your Wi-Fi. Away from home, the server needs Plex's Remote Access turned on, which streams through Plex as in any Plex app.

How do I sign in on Apple TV?

Ithaca shows a code. On your phone or computer, go to plex.tv/link while signed in to Plex and enter it. On iPhone and iPad you sign in to Plex in the sheet that opens.

Why does a file play through the server instead of directly?

Ithaca plays the original file whenever the device can: Dolby Vision, HDR10+, Dolby Atmos and every subtitle track. A few formats it hands to your Plex server to convert, such as Dolby TrueHD audio or Dolby Vision profile 5, and it does so without re-encoding the video when it can. You can always pick a quality yourself from the player.

Ithaca Pro and restoring a purchase

Ithaca Pro unlocks Describe It in Pick Something. It starts with a free 7-day trial, then it's a one-time purchase, with no subscription. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Apple TV signed in to the same Apple Account, and Family Sharing covers everyone at home. On a new device, or after reinstalling, open Settings → Ithaca Pro and tap Restore Purchase.

How does Describe It work?

Type what you're in the mood for, like "a heist movie under two hours", and Ithaca picks from your library. It runs on your device: on iPhone and iPad with Apple Intelligence turned on, Apple's on-device model reads what you typed; everywhere else, Ithaca's own matching does. With Seerr connected, it also suggests new titles to request.

To have Claude answer instead, run Athena on your Plex machine with your own Anthropic API key. Ithaca finds it there by itself, and anyone you share your Plex library with can use it too.

Requests with Seerr

If you run Seerr (or Overseerr or Jellyseerr) beside Plex, add it in Settings → Seerr and tap Connect with Plex. Search then shows titles you don't have yet, and you can request them from Ithaca. Running on the same machine as Plex, it's found without typing an address.

Is my viewing sent anywhere?

Not to us. Ithaca talks to Plex and to the servers you connect it to, and nothing goes to Inceptyon Labs. Details in the privacy policy.

Is Ithaca made by Plex?

No. Ithaca is an independent app by Inceptyon Labs and is not made or endorsed by Plex. Your Plex server stays the place to add media and manage libraries and users.
